# StarNet Corporate AI Training Offer

## Positioning

StarNet Technologies provides highly customized AI training for corporations
in Dubai and the UAE. Training is designed around the organization's sector,
business priorities, roles, workflows, systems, governance requirements, and
desired outcomes.

## Offer categories

### Exclusive one-to-one C-suite AI briefing

For a CEO, CFO, COO, CIO, CHRO, CDO, business-unit leader, or senior executive.

Typical themes:

- strategic AI opportunities and risks;
- investment and prioritization;
- executive decision-making and productivity;
- governance, privacy, security, and responsible AI;
- practical 30-60-90 day roadmap.

### Executive leadership workshop

For an executive committee or leadership team.

Typical themes:

- common AI language;
- enterprise use-case prioritization;
- operating-model implications;
- cross-functional collaboration;
- governance and accountability;
- value measurement.

### Department-head enablement

For heads of HR, finance, sales, marketing, operations, procurement, supply
chain, IT, risk, compliance, customer service, projects, or L&D.

Typical themes:

- function-specific use cases;
- workflow redesign;
- decision support;
- KPI improvement;
- team adoption planning.

### Customized team training

For operational and professional teams.

Typical themes:

- role-based prompting;
- research, analysis, drafting, and reporting;
- Microsoft 365 Copilot or other approved tools;
- department-specific exercises;
- safe and responsible use.

### Train-the-trainer and AI champions

For internal L&D teams, academies, transformation teams, and selected champions.

Typical themes:

- internal enablement framework;
- use-case and prompt libraries;
- facilitation;
- governance escalation;
- adoption measurement.

## Customization principles

A strong opportunity allows customization by industry, executive audience,
department, job role, process, platform, governance constraints, and measurable
business outcomes.
