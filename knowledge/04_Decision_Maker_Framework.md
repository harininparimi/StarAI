# Decision-Maker Framework

## Primary roles

- Chief Human Resources Officer;
- Chief Learning Officer;
- Head or Director of Learning and Development;
- Head of Talent Development;
- Head of Leadership Development;
- Head of Organizational Development;
- Corporate Academy Director;
- Head of Future Skills;
- Head of Workforce Transformation;
- Chief Digital Officer;
- Chief Data Officer;
- Chief AI Officer;
- Chief Information Officer;
- Head of Digital Transformation;
- Head of Innovation;
- Head of AI, Data, Analytics, or Automation;
- functional or business-unit head sponsoring the need.

## Secondary influencers

- HR business partners;
- employee-experience leaders;
- transformation-program leaders;
- strategy leaders;
- responsible-AI, risk, privacy, compliance, and cybersecurity leaders;
- procurement or vendor-management functions.

## Verification rules

A person may be included only when:

1. the person's name is visible on a public source;
2. the current role is clearly stated;
3. the role is relevant;
4. the source URL is recorded;
5. no identity, title, or URL is guessed.

## Acceptable public routes

- official company leadership page;
- official event speaker page;
- corporate announcement;
- public professional profile;
- credible industry association page;
- official company contact page;
- official procurement or supplier page;
- official switchboard;
- publicly stated professional email.

## Prohibited contact data

- guessed email formats;
- private phone numbers;
- personal addresses;
- data from private lead databases;
- unrelated personal information.
