- UAE corporate generative AI initiative;
- Dubai enterprise digital transformation;
- UAE corporate future skills;
- Dubai corporate upskilling program;
- UAE corporate academy;
- Dubai leadership development program;
- Dubai workforce transformation;

## Decision-maker searches

- company name chief digital officer;
- company name chief data officer;

## Trigger searches

- AI announcement;
- Copilot deployment;
- automation program;
- analytics transformation;
- executive appointment;
- academy launch;
- leadership-development program;
- employee upskilling;
- digital-skills initiative;
- AI governance framework;
- innovation lab;
- AI event or hackathon;
- relevant job openings.

## Research sequence

1. Verify company scale and UAE presence.
2. Find AI or digital signals.
3. Find learning or workforce signals.
4. Identify a recent trigger.
5. Identify relevant decision-maker functions.
6. Verify named people using public sources.
7. Find an official organizational route.
8. Score using the approved framework.
9. Export for human review.
