# Output Specification

Each lead should contain:

1. Rank
2. Company
3. Sector
4. Location
5. Company scale evidence
6. AI or digital signal
7. Learning or workforce signal
8. Recommended customized training opportunity
9. Recommended audiences
10. Trigger event
11. Decision-maker name
12. Decision-maker title
13. Public professional profile URL
14. Official public company contact route
15. Criterion scores
16. Total score
17. Tier
18. Recommendation
19. Source URLs
20. Validation status

## Rules

- Keep evidence concise.
- Separate evidence from recommendation.
- Use exact URLs.
- Use "Not Available" when unsupported.
- Do not include guessed personal information.
- Do not claim confirmed interest.
- Do not write outreach messages.
- Export for human review only.
