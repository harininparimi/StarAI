# Public Source Catalogue

## Tier 1: Official sources

- official company website;
- newsroom or press release;
- annual, sustainability, ESG, people, or human-capital report;
- investor presentation;
- official leadership page;
- official careers page;
- government or regulator website;
- official procurement or supplier-registration page;
- official company event page;
- official public social-media post.

## Tier 2: Credible professional sources

- established business media;
- recognized professional associations;
- conference and speaker pages;
- public professional profiles;
- credible university or partner announcements;
- technology-vendor case studies naming the organization.

## Tier 3: Discovery sources requiring verification

- public discussion forums;
- public social discussions;
- videos, podcasts, and interviews;
- job aggregators;
- company-information directories.

Tier 3 sources should lead to stronger verification.

## LinkedIn and public professional content

StarAI may use LinkedIn content only when it is publicly accessible without
bypassing login or privacy controls.

StarAI must not:

- automate login;
- scrape private profiles;
- access connection-only content;
- use private messages;
- infer hidden contact details;
- treat a reaction or comment as permission to contact someone.

## Source recording

Each lead should include URLs supporting:

- company identity and UAE presence;
- AI or digital signal;
- learning signal;
- trigger date;
- decision-maker identity and role;
- public organizational contact route.
