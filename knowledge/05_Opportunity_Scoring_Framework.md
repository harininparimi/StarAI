# Opportunity Scoring Framework

Maximum score: 100.

## Corporate scale and Dubai/UAE relevance — 20

- 20: Major UAE/Dubai corporate or large regional group with clear local
  operations.
- 15: Substantial enterprise with meaningful UAE presence.
- 8: Medium organization or weakly supported local relevance.
- 0: Unsupported or outside target market.

## AI or digital signal — 20

- 20: Multiple recent, specific AI, automation, data, Copilot, digital, or
  responsible-AI initiatives.
- 15: One strong recent initiative.
- 8: Broad digital-transformation signal.
- 0: No supported signal.

## Learning signal — 15

- 15: Recent L&D, academy, capability-building, upskilling, future-skills, or
  workforce-transformation activity.
- 10: General talent-development evidence.
- 5: Weak or old signal.
- 0: No supported signal.

## Training fit — 15

- 15: Clear fit for customized C-suite and departmental or team training.
- 10: Plausible fit supported by organization profile.
- 5: Generic fit only.
- 0: No fit.

## Decision-maker accessibility — 10

- 10: Current named relevant leader with verified public source.
- 7: Relevant leader or function is public, but evidence is incomplete.
- 4: Only a general organizational route is available.
- 0: No public route.

## Trigger recency — 10

- 10: Strong relevant trigger within 90 days.
- 7: Trigger within 12 months.
- 3: Older or undated evidence.
- 0: No trigger.

## Source quality — 5

- 5: Primarily official, regulatory, annual-report, government, or verified
  organizational sources.
- 3: Credible media, association, event, or public professional sources.
- 1: Weak secondary sources only.
- 0: Unsupported.

## Strategic value — 5

- 5: High-value multi-audience opportunity covering executives and teams.
- 4: Strong single-audience opportunity.
- 2: Limited pilot opportunity.
- 0: Poor alignment.

## Tiering

- Tier A+: 85-100 — Priority human review.
- Tier A: 75-84 — Proceed to human review.
- Tier B: 65-74 — Proceed after evidence check.
- Tier C: 50-64 — Further research required.
- Reject: below 50 — Do not prioritize.

A score is not proof of buying intent.
