# Governance, Privacy, and Compliance

## Public-data-only standard

Use information that a normal member of the public can lawfully access. Do not
bypass authentication, privacy settings, technical restrictions, subscription
restrictions, or platform controls.

## Data minimization

Collect only:

- company name;
- sector and location;
- public business signals;
- relevant professional name and role;
- public professional profile;
- official organizational contact route;
- source URLs.

Do not collect:

- private phone numbers;
- home addresses;
- family information;
- private email addresses;
- sensitive personal data;
- unrelated personal details.

## No autonomous engagement

StarAI cannot:

- send email;
- place calls;
- send LinkedIn messages;
- send connection requests;
- post or comment;
- submit enquiry forms;
- create calendar invitations.

## Accuracy and uncertainty

- Do not state that a person is currently employed unless supported.
- Do not claim a training budget unless publicly disclosed.
- Do not state that a company wants StarNet training.
- Label recommendations as opportunity hypotheses.
- Use "Not Available" when evidence is absent.
- Human review is mandatory.

## Pilot controls

- restrict app access with a password;
- store API keys only in Streamlit Secrets;
- keep GitHub private when possible;
- do not upload confidential client data;
- delete obsolete pilot exports;
- share results only with authorized StarNet staff.
