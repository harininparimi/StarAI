# Human Review SOP

Before any sales action, a StarNet reviewer must:

1. open and verify the key source URLs;
2. confirm Dubai or UAE operations;
3. confirm the AI, digital, learning, or workforce trigger;
4. confirm the trigger date;
5. confirm the decision-maker's current professional role;
6. remove unsupported or unnecessary personal information;
7. verify the organizational contact route;
8. confirm the proposed training is within StarNet capability;
9. approve, reject, or request further research;
10. record the review date and reviewer outside StarAI.

## Outcomes

### Approved for sales planning

Evidence is current and sufficient. A human may create a separate outreach plan.

### Further research

A key source, role, trigger, or contact route is missing.

### Rejected

The account is outside profile, evidence is weak, or there is privacy,
compliance, or reputational risk.
